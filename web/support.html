常見問題 FAQ

點樣取消「巴士預報-MBKa贊助人」訂閱？ How do I cancel the supporter subscription?
iPhone：設定 › [你的名稱] › 訂閱；Android：Google Play › 付款和訂閱。取消後會於當期完結時停止。
iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. It stops at the end of the current period.

換咗新手機點樣恢復訂閱？ Restore on a new phone?
App 內「設定 › 回復已購買項目」。 In the app: Settings › Restore Purchases.

點解 iPhone 收唔到上落車提醒？ Why no boarding/get-off alerts on iPhone?
iOS 版提醒需要保持 App 開啟使用中。 On iOS, alerts work while the app is open and in use.
